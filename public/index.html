Uncle Pie's Storybook
How it works How it's built

Bedtime stories for ages 5 to 10

Tell Uncle Pie an idea.
Watch it become a picture book.

Type it, say it out loud, or show a photo of your favourite people and pets. Uncle Pie writes the story, his editor Vishnu checks every page, and the pictures paint themselves while Uncle Pie reads to you.

Three ways to begin

Type it

One line or a whole plot. "A bear and a fox who don't get along..." Uncle Pie keeps every idea you give him.

Say it

Tap the microphone and tell the story roughly, the way kids do. Uncle Pie listens and tidies it up.

Show it

Add up to three photos: your child, a grandparent, the family cat. The characters will be inspired by them.

Inside the workshop

Every story goes through the same careful steps. You can watch them happen.

  1. Youidea, voice or photos
  2. Uncle Pie listensfinds the kind of story, your must-have moments, and the lesson
  3. Uncle Pie plansa five-beat arc with your ideas pinned to pages
  4. Uncle Pie writesshort read-aloud sentences, dialogue, a refrain to join in on
  5. Vishnu reviewsseven checks, quote-verified fidelity, reading-level maths. Sends notes back until it's right
  6. Painted & narratedink lines, then watercolour, then Uncle Pie's voice

Photos are sent to OpenAI only to draw your book. This app never stores them.
