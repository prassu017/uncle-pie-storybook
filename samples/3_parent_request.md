# Robbie's Thunderstorm Adventure

**Request:** My son is scared of thunderstorms. Make a story where a little robot learns that storms are loud but they pass, and his grandma teaches him a breathing trick.

*Category: calm · 5 pages · text ready in 84s · reading grade 4.3 · Vishnu: passed on draft 2*

**Ideas kept from the request (checked by quote):** ✅ Robbie is scared of thunderstorms; ✅ Grandma Bot teaches Robbie a breathing trick to stay calm during storms; ✅ Robbie practices the breathing trick during a big thunderstorm; ✅ Robbie realizes that storms are loud but they pass

**Page 1.** Raindrops tapped on the window, making Robbie's eyes as big as dinner plates. 'Thunderstorm!' he whispered, his lights flickering nervously. With a beep, he scurried to the cozy robot workshop, hiding under a metal table. Anxious, he peeked out, watching the flashes in the sky. Suddenly, a loud rumble echoed through the workshop, and Robbie shivered, his fear of thunderstorms growing. 'Breathe in, breathe out,' he whispered to himself, trying to calm down.

**Page 2.** Grandma Bot noticed Robbie's fear and knelt down beside him, her kind eyes filled with understanding. 'Robbie, dear, are you scared of the thunderstorm?' she asked gently. Robbie nodded, his round eyes wide with worry. Grandma Bot smiled warmly, patting his metal back. 'I have a special trick that might help you feel better,' she said softly. Her voice was like a soothing melody. Robbie looked up at her, feeling hopeful.

**Page 3.** Grandma Bot sat next to Robbie, smiling kindly. 'Let's try this,' she said. She took a big breath, then a slow one. 'Breathe in, breathe out,' she whispered. Robbie copied her, looking interested. They breathed in and out, feeling calm. 'Great job, Robbie,' Grandma Bot said, patting his back gently. Robbie's eyes sparkled, eager to learn more.

**Page 4.** The thunder growled outside. Robbie sat in the workshop, remembering Grandma Bot's trick. Taking a deep breath, he closed his eyes. 'Breathe in, breathe out,' he whispered. Lightning flashed, but Robbie stayed calm, feeling brave. His eyes sparkled with courage. With each breath, he felt stronger. The storm raged on, but Robbie sat tall, mastering his fear. Grandma Bot's trick was his shield, making him feel invincible.

**Page 5.** The storm slowly quieted down, the thunder softening to a distant rumble. Robbie sighed contentedly, his lights glowing gently. 'Grandma Bot,' he said, 'I'm not scared anymore. Storms pass.' With a smile, Grandma Bot nodded. 'That's right, Robbie. Breathe in, breathe out.' Robbie closed his eyes, feeling peaceful and strong. The rain pattered softly outside as Robbie sat calmly, a little robot in the quiet workshop.

*The moral:* Robbie learned storms are loud but go away, and we can feel calm.
