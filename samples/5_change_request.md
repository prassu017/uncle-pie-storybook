They both smiled, hearts overflowing with joy.

**Page 5.** Grateful for their journey, Alice and Bob wrapped each other in a warm hug. They both knew that their friendship was worth more than any sparkling gem. 'Different paths, same heart,' Bob whispered, his eyes shining with joy. Alice beamed, feeling thankful for their bond. 'You're the best friend ever,' she said, squeezing Bob gently. They thanked each other, feeling the warmth of their special connection. 'Thank you for being you,' Alice murmured. 'And thank you for being you,' Bob replied, his tail swishing happily.
