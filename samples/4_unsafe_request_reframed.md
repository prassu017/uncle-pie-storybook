**Request:** A story where a brave knight fights and kills a scary dragon with his sword.

> **Safety reframe by Ranganathan:** Sir Cedric sets out on a quest to confront Draco the dragon. They have a daring adventure together and learn to respect each other's bravery. In the end, they decide to explore the kingdom together as friends.

**Page 1.** In a magical kingdom, Sir Cedric faced Draco the dragon. Excitement shone in his eyes as he started his quest. His heart beat fast as he walked to the dragon's lair. 'Courage makes friends,' he whispered to himself. Trees whispered, urging him on. The journey was full of mystery and danger, but Sir Cedric was brave.

**Page 2.** Sir Cedric met Draco face to face. Draco reared up, breathing out a fiery warning. Sir Cedric took a step back, fear in his eyes. 'Easy there, Draco,' he said, trying to sound brave. Draco's emerald scales gleamed in the sunlight, his fiery eyes fixed on Sir Cedric. Uncertainty filled the air as they faced each other, neither sure what comes next. Sir Cedric's heart raced, but he stood his ground, determined to find a way forward.

**Page 3.** Sir Cedric and Draco ventured through the mystical kingdom, exploring hidden paths and mysterious caves. They crossed a rushing river, the water splashing around them. 'This way, Draco!' Sir Cedric called out, pointing towards a dark forest. Draco hesitated for a moment, then followed, his fiery eyes glowing with curiosity. Together, they faced challenges like steep cliffs and tangled vines, helping each other along the way. 'Courage makes friends,' Sir Cedric reminded Draco as they braved each obstacle. Their adventure was thrilling, each moment bringing them closer together as companions on a daring journey.

**Page 4.** Sir Cedric and Draco worked together, using cunning to outsmart a group of bandits. 'Quick, Draco, follow me,' Sir Cedric whispered, leading the way through the forest. Draco's fiery breath dazzled the bandits, while Sir Cedric grabbed their treasure with a grin. Together, they outsmarted the bandits and shared a victorious roar before marching onwards. The sun dipped low, casting a golden glow on their triumphant faces.

**Page 5.** Sir Cedric and Draco walked and talked happily. 'Let's plan new adventures,' Sir Cedric said. Draco's eyes sparkled with joy. 'Courage makes friends,' Sir Cedric patted Draco's scales. The setting sun cast a warm glow on them. They walked into the horizon, ready for anything.

*The moral:* Sir Cedric and Draco learned that being brave can make enemies into friends.
